Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how AgroDrone Finland Oy uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Effective date: 2026-08-06 Company: AgroDrone Finland Oy

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work correctly, remember preferences, and provide usage insights.

2. Cookie categories we use

  • Necessary cookies: Required for core website functionality and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve performance and content.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to measure campaign effectiveness and improve ad relevance.

3. How consent works on this site

When you first visit the site, you can accept all cookies, reject optional cookies, or choose custom settings. Your preferences are saved in your browser and can be updated at any time through "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

Necessary cookies remain active at all times because they are required for essential site operations.

4. Managing and deleting cookies

You can manage your preferences using the cookie controls on this website, and you can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may impact site functionality.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at info@agrodrone.fi.
